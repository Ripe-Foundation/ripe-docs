# Audits

These published reports document independent security reviews of specific Ripe Protocol code snapshots. Their scope does not automatically extend to later changes or deployments.

## ChainSecurity

[Read the ChainSecurity report](https://www.hightop.com/docs/resources/audits/ChainSecurity-Ripe.pdf)

* **Reviewed versions:** Nine legacy Ripe Finance smart-contract revisions received from January 10, 2024 through January 17, 2025
* **Scope:** Smart contracts under `contracts/`. Mock and configuration files and economic modeling were excluded.

## Anatomist

[Read the Anatomist report](https://www.hightop.com/docs/resources/audits/Anatomist-Ripe.pdf)

* **Report date:** November 7, 2025
* **Assessment period:** October 6–31, 2025
* **Reviewed revision:** `d5a315e509837192d57ea5789278b7ff2b27a6f2`
* **Scope:** Core Ripe Protocol functionality. Changes after the assessment period were excluded.

Audits are not a guarantee of safety. They are one input to consider when evaluating protocol risk.
